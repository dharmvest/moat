## 一、【故事】STORY

### S1. 1964 年 5 月 6 日：八分之一点的傲慢，与一辆被狗追上的车
- **1964 年 5 月 6 日**，当时由 **Seabury Stanton** 掌管的伯克希尔致信股东，提出以 **每股 $11.375** 回购 **225,000 股**。巴菲特说："I had expected the letter; I was surprised by the price."（我预料到会有这封信，但价格让我意外。）
- 当时伯克希尔流通股 **1,583,680 股**，其中约 **7%** 由 **Buffett Partnership Ltd.（BPL）** 持有——那是巴菲特管理、且几乎是他全部身家的投资实体。
- 在要约信寄出前不久，Stanton 问巴菲特 BPL 愿意以什么价卖出。巴菲特答 **$11.50**。Stanton 说："**Fine, we have a deal.**"（好，成交。）然后伯克希尔的信来了，报价低了 **八分之一美元（an eighth of a point）**。
- 巴菲特被这种"抠门"激怒（"I bristled at Stanton's behavior"），**拒绝出售**。
- 他的自评：**"That was a monumentally stupid decision."**（那是个愚蠢至极的决定。）
- 后续连锁：巴菲特转而** aggressively 买入更多伯克希尔股票**。到 **1965 年 4 月**，BPL 已持有 **392,633 股**（当时流通 **1,017,547 股**）；**1965 年 5 月初**的董事会上正式取得控制权。
- 结局：**"Through Seabury's and my childish behavior – after all, what was an eighth of a point to either of us? – he lost his job, and I found myself with more than 25% of BPL's capital invested in a terrible business about which I knew very little. I became the dog who caught the car."**（因为 Seabury 和我孩子气的行为——毕竟，八分之一美元对我们俩算得了什么？——他丢了工作，而我发现自己把 BPL 超过 25% 的资本，投进了一门我所知甚少的糟糕生意。我成了那只追上汽车的狗。）
- 背景数字：1955 年合并时净资产 **$55 million**；到 **1964 财年末**跌至 **$22 million**，且这 22 百万全被纺织业务占用，公司**没有多余现金，还欠银行 $2.5 million**。
- 1954 年 7 月 29 日的伯克希尔董事会纪要原文：**"The textile industry in New England started going out of business forty years ago. During the war years this trend was stopped. The trend must continue until supply and demand have been balanced."**
- **巴菲特 1962 年 12 月**首次买入伯克希尔：股价 **$7.50**，每股营运资金 **$10.25**，账面价值 **$20.20**。他的比喻：**"like picking up a discarded cigar butt that had one puff remaining in it. Though the stub might be ugly and soggy, the puff would be free."**
- 1955 年合并：**Berkshire Fine Spinning Associates** 与 **Hathaway Manufacturing**（两家都源自 19 世纪）合并，**14 家工厂、10,000 名员工**，成为新英格兰纺织业巨头。巴菲特的判词：**"What the two managements viewed as a merger agreement, however, soon morphed into a suicide pact."**（两家管理层眼中的合并协议，很快变成了一纸自杀协定。）合并后七年间整体亏损，**净资产缩水 37%**，关闭 9 家工厂。
- 收尾：婚后期只有两年好日子（且有巨额亏损结转，那两年**免税**）；**1966 年之后的 18 年**"we struggled unremittingly with the textile business, all to no avail"；**1985 年**终于关门。

### S2. 1967：Jack Ringwalt 的十五分钟，与一份没有律师的合同
- **1967 年**，巴菲特让伯克希尔花 **$8.6 million** 买下 **National Indemnity Company（NICO）** 及其一家小姊妹公司（National Fire & Marine）。奥马哈本地企业。
- 卖家 **Jack Ringwalt** 是巴菲特的老朋友，**他要卖给巴菲特本人，而不是伯克希尔**。巴菲特："In no way was his offer intended for Berkshire."
- 过程：**"Jack Ringwalt... came to my office saying he would like to sell. Fifteen minutes later, we had a deal."**
- **两家公司从未经公共会计师事务所审计过，巴菲特也没要审计。** 他的理由有两条，写得很干脆：**"(1) Jack was honest and (2) He was also a bit quirky and likely to walk away if the deal became at all complicated."**
- 合同：**1 又 1/2 页的购买协议**（原件影印在 PDF 第 128-129 页），**"That contract was homemade: Neither side used a lawyer."** 巴菲特评：**"Per page, this has to be Berkshire's best deal"**——今天 National Indemnity 的 GAAP 净资产达 **$111 billion**，超过世界上任何其他保险公司。
- **同一故事的两面**：巴菲特把这次收购称为他一生中**最昂贵的错误**（详见 S3）。

### S3. 巴菲特自认一生最贵的错误：把钱买错了"壳"
- 1967 年，巴菲特**让伯克希尔（而非 BPL）**去买 NICO。他写道：**"So why did I purchase NICO for Berkshire rather than for BPL? I've had 48 years to think about that question, and I've yet to come up with a good answer. I simply made a colossal mistake."**
- 后果的量化推演（这是全信最重的忏悔）：若 BPL 是买方，他的合伙人将 **100% 拥有一家好生意**；成长不会被纺织业务里"被囚禁的"无生产力资金拖累近二十年；后续所有收购都将由他和合伙人**全部拥有**，而不是被伯克希尔遗留股东分走 **39%**——"to whom we had no obligation"（我们对他们本无义务）。
- 最终判词：**"I opted to marry 100% of an excellent business (NICO) to a 61%-owned terrible business (Berkshire Hathaway), a decision that eventually diverted $100 billion or so from BPL partners to a collection of strangers."**（我选择把一门 100% 的好生意，嫁给一门我只拥有 61% 的烂生意，这个决定最终把大约 1000 亿美元从 BPL 合伙人手里，转给了一群陌生人。）

### S4. 1975 年，他又买了一家纺织厂
- **"One more confession and then I'll go on to more pleasant topics: Can you believe that in 1975 I bought Waumbec Mills, another New England textile company?"**
- 理由听起来很合理：价格相对资产是"bargain"，且能与伯克希尔现有纺织业务产生"projected synergies"（预期协同）。结果：**"surprise, surprise – Waumbec was a disaster, with the mill having to be closed down not many years later."**
- 自嘲收尾：**"And now some good news: The northern textile industry is finally extinct. You need no longer panic if you hear that I've been spotted wandering around New England."**

### S5. 1951：把 65% 身家押进 GEICO 的那个下午
- **1951 年 1 月**，巴菲特第一次接触 GEICO，被其相对行业巨头的巨大成本优势震住：**"It was clear to me that GEICO would succeed because it deserved to succeed."**
- 关键人物：**Lorimer Davidson**（"a wonderful man who later became CEO of the company"），1951 年的一次谈话；谈完，巴菲特把 **$9,800 净身家的 65%** 投进 GEICO 股票。
- 他写下了汽车险这门生意的底层逻辑：**"No one likes to buy auto insurance. Almost everyone, though, likes to drive."**

### S6. 1959：奥马哈的医生做了一次介绍
- Charlie 小时候住在巴菲特现在住所**几百英尺**外；少年时代，两人**都在巴菲特的祖父的杂货店里打工过**。但两人直到 **1959 年**才见面——巴菲特 **28 岁**，芒格 **35 岁**，芒格早已离开奥马哈定居洛杉矶。
- **"The Omaha doctor who introduced us predicted that we would hit it off – and we did."**
- 56 年的相处法则（一条绝妙的对话）：**"In 56 years, however, we've never had an argument. When we differ, Charlie usually ends the conversation by saying: 'Warren, think it over and you'll agree with me because you're smart and I'm right.'"**
- 芒格的前史：**执业律师，时薪 $15**；30 多岁时靠**在洛杉矶附近设计并建造五栋公寓项目**赚到第一笔真正的钱；**同时设计了自己此后住了 55 年的房子**；近年设计了**斯坦福和密歇根大学的大型宿舍群**；现年 **91 岁**，仍在做一个大项目。
- 巴菲特对芒格一生"建筑作品"的排序：**"From my perspective, though, Charlie's most important architectural feat was the design of today's Berkshire."**（但在我看来，查理最重要的建筑作品，是设计了今天的伯克希尔。）

### S7. 1972：See's Candy，卖方多要了 500 万，买方少给了 500 万
- **1972 年**，通过 **Blue Chip Stamps**（芒格、巴菲特、伯克希尔都重仓，后并入伯克希尔）买下 See's。
- 当时 See's 每年税前赚约 **$4 million**，只占用 **$8 million** 净有形资产。它有一项**不在资产负债表上**的资产：广泛而持久的竞争优势，带定价权。
- 控股家族要价 **$30 million**，**芒格说值这个价**。巴菲特：**"But I didn't want to pay more than $25 million and wasn't all that enthusiastic even at that figure. (A price that was three times net tangible assets made me gulp.)"**——他自己承认：**"My misguided caution could have scuttled a terrific purchase."**
- 结局：卖方接受了 **$25 million**。
- 50 年的复利账：**See's 累计税前赚了 $1.9 billion，而支撑这些增长只需追加投入 $40 million**。它的比喻：**"(Envision rabbits breeding.)"**（想象兔子繁殖。）
- 附带收益：**"through watching See's in action, I gained a business education about the value of powerful brands that opened my eyes to many other profitable investments."**

### S8. 1993：Dexter Shoe——一只鞋蒸发了 57 亿
- **1993 年**买入 Dexter Shoe。"it had a terrific record and in no way looked to me like a cigar butt."（业绩优异，怎么看都不像雪茄烟蒂。）它的竞争优势因**外国竞争**而迅速蒸发，**"And I simply didn't see that coming."**
- 数字：伯克希尔付了 **$433 million**，价值**很快归零**。
- 真正的伤口（GAAP 不会记录）：**"The fact is that I gave Berkshire stock to the sellers of Dexter rather than cash, and the shares I used for the purchase are now worth about $5.7 billion. As a financial disaster, this one deserves a spot in the Guinness Book of World Records."**
- 抽出的通则：**"Trading shares of a wonderful business – which Berkshire most certainly is – for ownership of a so-so business irreparably destroys value."**

### S9. Tesco：一只蟑螂，与它的亲戚们
- **2012 年底**持有 **415 million 股** Tesco（英国领先食品零售商），成本 **$2.3 billion**，市值相当。
- **2013 年**："I soured somewhat on the company's then-management and sold 114 million shares, realizing a profit of $43 million."（我对其当时的管理层有些失望，卖掉 1.14 亿股，实现利润 4300 万美元。）但卖得太慢、太从容。
- **"Charlie calls this sort of behavior 'thumb-sucking.' (Considering what my delay cost us, he is being kind.)"**（查理把这种行为叫"嘬手指"。考虑到我的拖延让我们付出了什么代价，他这句话已经很客气了。）
- **2014 年**：市场份额下滑，利润率收缩，**会计问题浮出水面**。巴菲特的句子：**"In the world of business, bad news often surfaces serially: You see a cockroach in your kitchen; as the days go by, you meet his relatives."**
- 结局：年内全部卖出，**税后亏损 $444 million**，约占伯克希尔净值的 **1/5 of 1%**。
- 历史坐标（罕见地给出：他一生只有三次大额实现亏损）：**"In the past 50 years, we have only once realized an investment loss that at the time of sale cost us 2% of our net worth. Twice, we experienced 1% losses. All three of these losses occurred in the 1974-1975 period, when we sold stocks that were very cheap in order to buy others we believed to be even cheaper."**
- 自评定性：**"An attentive investor, I'm embarrassed to report, would have sold Tesco shares earlier. I made a big mistake with this investment by dawdling."**

### S10. 已故的 Jimmy Ling：Meatball, Golf Ball and Goof Ball
- **Jimmy Ling**：靠一连串公司杂耍，把 LTV 从 **1965 年 $36 million 销售额**做到**两年后《财富》500 强第 14 位**。
- 巴菲特的评语（引自芒格）：**"Ling, it should be noted, had never displayed any managerial skills. But Charlie told me long ago to never underestimate the man who overestimates himself. And Ling had no peer in that respect."**（永远不要低估一个高估自己的人——而在这方面凌格无人能及。）
- 招法名叫 **"project redeployment"**（项目再部署）：买下一家大公司，再把各分部逐一分拆上市。**1966 年年报**原文：**"Most importantly, acquisitions must meet the test of the 2 plus 2 equals 5 (or 6) formula."**
- **1967 年**买下大肉联厂 **Wilson & Co.**（兼有高尔夫器材和制药业务），随即把母公司一拆为三：Wilson & Co.（肉）、Wilson Sporting Goods（球具）、Wilson Pharmaceuticals（药）。华尔街当天给它们起了绰号：**Meatball, Golf Ball and Goof Ball**（肉丸、高尔夫球、糊涂丸）。
- 结局：**"Soon thereafter, it became clear that, like Icarus, Ling had flown too close to the sun. By the early 1970s, Ling's empire was melting, and he himself had been spun off from LTV . . . that is, fired."**（凌格像伊卡洛斯飞得太靠近太阳……他自己也被从 LTV"分拆"了出去——也就是，被解雇了。）
- 出处提示：**D Magazine 1982 年 10 月号**那篇写 Jimmy Ling 的文章，"those who enjoy a good financial story should read the piece... Look it up on the Internet."

### S11. 1960 年代末：一个吹嘘"大胆、富有想象力会计"的 CEO
- **"In the late 1960s, I attended a meeting at which an acquisitive CEO bragged of his 'bold, imaginative accounting.' Most of the analysts listening responded with approving nods, seeing themselves as having found a manager whose forecasts were certain to be met, whatever the business results might be."**
- 那套手法被完整拆解（可作为 1960 年代并购泡沫的教科书）：把股价推到 20 倍市盈率 → 尽快发股票去收购 10 倍市盈率的公司 → 立刻用 **"pooling"（权益合并法）**会计 → **底层业务一分钱没变，每股收益自动上升** → 把上升当作管理天才的证据 → 告诉投资者这种天才应维持甚至提升市盈率 → 承诺无限重复。
- 被神化的名字：**ITT、Litton Industries、Gulf & Western、LTV**。巴菲特引用 **Yogi Berra**：**"Every Napoleon meets his Watergate."**（每个拿破仑都会遇上自己的水门事件。）
- 结局句：**"Eventually, however, the clock struck twelve, and everything turned to pumpkins and mice."**（终于，午夜钟响，一切变回南瓜和老鼠。）

### S12. 1979：被迫剥离的罗克福德银行
- 伯克希尔唯一一次真实分拆：**1979 年**，银行控股公司新规迫使伯克希尔剥离其在**伊利诺伊州罗克福德（Rockford, Illinois）**拥有的一家银行。

### S13. 2014 年新增：Van Tuyl 汽车经销集团
- **2014 年 10 月**签约收购 **Van Tuyl Automotive**——**78 家汽车经销商**，巴菲特称之为特优运营。
- 缘起：**"Larry Van Tuyl, the company's owner, and I met some years ago. He then decided that if he were ever to sell his company, its home should be Berkshire."**（如果他有一天要卖，它的归宿应该是伯克希尔。）
- **Larry 和父亲 Cecil 用 62 年**建起这个集团，策略是**让所有地方经理都成为"owner-partners"（所有者-合伙人）**："Creating this mutuality of interests proved over and over to be a winner."
- 现状：全美第五大汽车经销集团，单店销售额极为出色。**Jeff Rachor** 近年与 Larry 并肩工作，安排将延续。
- 行业结构：美国约有 **17,000 家经销商**，**股权转让一律需相关车厂批准**。巴菲特的战略是：做到让车厂欢迎伯克希尔继续收购。
- 野心：**"we will build a business that before long will be multiples the size of Van Tuyl's $9 billion of sales."**

### S14. 2014 年最大败笔：BNSF 让客户失望
- **"Our bad news from 2014 comes from our group of five as well and is unrelated to earnings. During the year, BNSF disappointed many of its customers. These shippers depend on us, and service failures can badly hurt their businesses."**
- 对照数据：伯克希尔与 **Union Pacific（UP）** 营收规模大致相当，但伯克希尔运量明显更大（按车皮数或吨英里）；**去年我们的服务问题比 UP 更严重，因此丢了市场份额**；**"Moreover, U.P.'s earnings beat ours by a record amount. Clearly, we have a lot of work to do."**
- 应对：2015 年投入 **$6 billion** 改善铁路运营，约为预估营收的 **26%**。对比：伯克希尔 2009-2013 年平均 **18%**，UP 未来预测 **16-17%**。**"That sum is nearly 50% more than any other railroad has spent in a single year."** 巴菲特承认："The extensive work required to increase system capacity sometimes disrupts operations while it is underway."
- 一线转机（信中原文）：**"Recently, however, our outsized expenditures are beginning to show results. During the last three months, BNSF's performance metrics have materially improved from last year's figures."**
- BNSF 的负债能力佐证：**"Last year, for example, BNSF's interest coverage was more than 8:1. (Our definition of coverage is pre-tax earnings/interest, not EBITDA/interest, a commonly used measure we view as seriously flawed.)"**

### S15. 2014 年 12 月的那张保单：$3 billion 单一保费
- **2014 年**承保了一张**单一保费 $3 billion** 的保单。巴菲特自述：这规模历史上**仅次于 2007 年与 Lloyd's（劳合社）的那笔交易，那笔保费 $7.1 billion**。
- 他的断言：**"In fact, I know of only eight P/C policies in history that had a single premium exceeding $1 billion. And, yes, all eight were written by Berkshire. Certain of these contracts will require us to make substantial payments 50 years or more from now. When major insurers have needed an unquestionable promise that payments of this type will be made, Berkshire has been the party – the only party – to call."**
- 交易动机（一段精妙的机制描述）：全球最大、最老练的几家财险公司想把巨额、超长尾的负债（**大多是石棉索赔的潜在损失**）"cede"（分出）给再保人。麻烦在于：**"If a reinsurer fails to pay a loss, the original insurer is still on the hook for it."**（再保人不赔，原保险人仍得兜着。）所以选再保人就是选"五十年后还活着且认账的人"。

### S16. 2014 年灾难假设的压力测试：$250 billion 巨灾
- **"if the insurance industry should experience a $250 billion loss from some mega-catastrophe – a loss about triple anything it has ever experienced – Berkshire as a whole would likely record a significant profit for the year because of its many streams of earnings. We would also remain awash in cash and be looking for large opportunities in a market that might well have gone into shock. Meanwhile, other major insurers and reinsurers would be far in the red, if not facing insolvency."**

### S17. 2008 年 9-10 月：三周，$15.6 billion
- **"At Berkshire, our 'breathing' went uninterrupted. Indeed, in a three-week period spanning late September and early October, we supplied $15.6 billion of fresh money to American businesses."**
- 时代的画面（可与雷曼时刻并读）：**"American business provided a case study of that in 2008. In September of that year, many long-prosperous companies suddenly wondered whether their checks would bounce in the days ahead. Overnight, their financial oxygen disappeared."**
- 支撑它的纪律：伯克希尔**永远持有至少 $20 billion**（通常远多于此）的现金等价物。

### S18. 2014 年 5 月 3 日：年度股东大会的全套人物与细节（可直接当场景写）
- **地点/时间**：CenturyLink Center，**5 月 2 日星期六**。去年出席 **39,000 人**创纪录，金婚年预计更多。**"Be there when the doors open at 7 a.m."**

### S25. 2014 年私募股权买家画像（与"伯克希尔的第三个选择"对照）

- 伯克希尔的第三条路：**"a permanent home, in which the company's people and culture will be retained... any business we acquire dramatically increases its financial strength and ability to grow. ## 二、【规则】RULE

**关于"过去 50 年"的叙事骨架**

- R1. 时间会奖励对的方向，而不是聪明的进出：50 年 19.4% 的复合增长率，来自"持有一组不断变好的生意"，而不是来自任何一年的择时——同期 S&P 500 只有 9.9%。
- R2. 记录真实价值的尺子必须随公司性质变化而更换：早年资产多按市价计价（marked to market），账面价值与内在价值贴近；如今重心转向整体收购的经营性企业，这些企业**永不被向上重估**，因此"the gap between Berkshire's intrinsic value and its book value has materially widened." —— **尺子不变，读数就会撒谎。**
- R3. 增发股票必须用"每股"而不是"总量"来检验：五家公司十年增加 **$12 billion** 年利润，而股本只被稀释 **6.1%**（仅 BNSF 一役）。**"That satisfies our goal of not simply increasing earnings, but making sure we also increase per-share results."**
- R4. 浮存金（float）的真实价值 = 无成本 + 长久。会计上把 float 全额记为负债（当作明天就要付出去且无法补充），但"Owing $1 that in effect will never leave the premises – because new business is almost certain to deliver a substitute – is worlds different from owing $1 that will go out the door tomorrow and not be replaced."
- R5. 保险经营的四条戒律，第四条最难：(1) 理解一切可能致损的风险敞口；(2) 保守评估其发生概率与可能代价；(3) 定价须在覆盖预期损失与运营费用后仍平均盈利；(4) **在拿不到合适价格时愿意走开**。**"Many insurers pass the first three tests and flunk the fourth."**
- R6. 决定长期结果的是"整体结构"而不是单笔交易：伯克希尔把"经营企业 + 被动持股"两条腿都放在同一个屋顶下，**"Our appetite for either operating businesses or passive investments doubles our chances of finding sensible uses for Berkshire's endless gusher of cash."**
- R7. 两种技能必须分开练：**评估投资**与**经营企业**是两种不同的能力，"some truths can only be fully learned through experience."
- R8. 保守的极端形式是可计算的：永远保留 **至少 $20 billion** 现金等价物（且必须是美国国库券）；不承担大额短期债务到期；不写需要大额追缴保证金的衍生品；不写允许保单持有人**自行选择退保套现**的保险合同（寿险的赎回条款会在极端恐慌中造成"挤兑"）。**"When bills come due, only cash is legal tender."**
- R9. 危机准备是一种机会：2008 年 9-10 月的三周里，伯克希尔向美国企业注入 **$15.6 billion**——**唯一能在恐慌中当买家，是因为平时不欠任何人。** "if it occurs we will be selling life jackets to the unprepared."
- R10. 好股权比好债权安全（长周期）：**1964-2014 年 S&P 500 从 84 涨到 2,059（含分红总回报 11,196%），同期美元购买力暴跌 87%**——"it now takes $1 to buy what could be bought for 13¢ in 1965"。因此**分散持有美国企业，比持有以美元计价的证券（如国债）安全得多**；且这在包含大萧条与两次世界大战的前一个半世纪同样成立。
- R10b. 投资的定义（引自 2011 年报，本信重述）：**"the transfer to others of purchasing power now with the reasoned expectation of receiving more purchasing power – after taxes have been paid on nominal gains – in the future."** 以此为尺，**"currency-denominated instruments are riskier investments – far riskier investments – than widely-diversified stock portfolios that are bought over time and that are owned in a manner invoking only token fees and commissions."** 而适用的前提是：**多十年的持有期**（"who can – and should – invest with a multi-decade horizon, quotational declines are unimportant"）。短期资金、投资银行这类"资产价格下跌会威胁生存"的主体，则应持有国库券或受保存款。
- R11. 波动不是风险。**"Volatility is far from synonymous with risk. Popular formulas that equate the two terms lead students, investors and CEOs astray."**
- R12. 投资者真正会毁掉自己收益的行为清单（信中原话）：**"Active trading, attempts to 'time' market movements, inadequate diversification, the payment of high and unnecessary fees to managers and advisors, and the use of borrowed money"**；并且**"borrowed money has no place in the investor's tool kit"**。
- R13. 机构跑不过散户指数基金的原因是费用链：**"Many institutions pay substantial sums to consultants who, in turn, recommend high-fee managers. And that is a fool's game."** "Most advisors, however, are far better at generating high fees than they are at generating high returns. In truth, their core competence is salesmanship."
- R14. 会计调整的原则：无形资产分两种——软件摊销是**真实成本**，客户关系摊销是**购买会计的产物、不反映现实**。**"When CEOs tout EBITDA as a valuation guide, wire them up for a polygraph test."** 而折旧不同：**"Every dime of depreciation expense we report is a real cost."**
- R15. 回购的唯一合法标准：只有当价格**远低于内在价值**时。**"Berkshire's directors will only authorize repurchases at a price they believe to be well below intrinsic value. (In our view, that is an essential criterion for repurchases that is often ignored by other managements.)"**
- R16. 好价格 ≠ 好投资：**"a business with terrific economics can be a bad investment if it is bought for too high a price."** 同理，**"a sound investment can morph into a rash speculation if it is bought at an elevated price."**
- R17. 买入的价格如果**接近两倍账面价值**（伯克希尔股价偶有到达），可能需要很多年才能获利。
- R18. 收购时：**"The intrinsic value of the shares you give in an acquisition must not be greater than the intrinsic value of the business you receive."** 并且**"You can't get rich trading a hundred-dollar bill for eight tens (even if your advisor has handed you an expensive 'fairness' opinion endorsing that swap)."**
- R19. 交易后复盘应当成为董事会标准动作：**"Post mortems of acquisitions, in which reality is honestly compared to the original projections, are rare in American boardrooms. They should instead be standard practice."**
- R20. 收购纪律（页面 23 的固定标准，50 年基本未变）：(1) 大额（税前利润至少 **$75 million**，除非能并入既有单元）；(2) 已证明的持续盈利能力（**不看预测，不碰困境反转**）；(3) 高净资产回报且少用或不用债务；(4) 管理层已在位（**"we can't supply it"**）；(5) 业务简单（**"if there's lots of technology, we won't understand it"**）；(6) 必须给出报价。**"We can promise complete confidentiality and a very fast answer – customarily within five minutes."** 不做敌意收购，不参与拍卖。附带一句乡村歌曲：**"When the phone don't ring, you'll know it's me."** 以及一个比喻：**"if you advertise an interest in buying collies, a lot of people will call hoping to sell you their cocker spaniels."**（如果你登广告说想买柯利牧羊犬，会有很多人打电话来想把他们的可卡犬卖给你。）
- R21. 增长有极限，且极限可提前宣告：**"Berkshire's long-term gains – measured by percentages, not by dollars – cannot be dramatic and will not come close to those achieved in the past 50 years. The numbers have become too big."**
- R22. 规模终将迫使分红或回购：**"Eventually – probably between ten and twenty years from now – Berkshire's earnings and capital resources will reach a level that will not allow management to intelligently reinvest all of the company's earnings."** 判断标准：**若股价低于内在价值，大规模回购几乎肯定是最佳选择。**
- R23. 选 CEO 的标准：**资本配置能力 + 选留优秀经理人的能力**。素质要求：理性、冷静、果断、对商业有广博理解、对人性有洞察，**并且知道自己的边界**（引 Tom Watson, Sr.：**"I'm no genius, but I'm smart in spots and I stay around those spots."**）。
- R24. 反 CEO 衰老机制的三条制度设计：候选人**来自内部且董事们早已熟悉**；**上任时相对年轻**；**CEO 平均在任远超十年**，且**不会 65 岁退休**。
- R25. 组织规模的反设计：总部**从未设过委员会**，**从未要求子公司提交预算**，且**没有法务部**，也没有人力资源、公关、投资者关系、战略、并购等部门。**"We do, of course, have an active audit function; no sense being a damned fool."**——授权是官僚主义的解药：**"In an operating sense, Berkshire is not a giant company but rather a collection of large companies."**

**关于"未来 50 年"的推演（信中对家人的口吻）**

- R26. 永久性资本损失的概率"as low as can be found among single-company investments"，因为**每股内在价值几乎必然随时间增长**。
- R27. 建议**至少持有五年**。**"Those who seek short-term profits should look elsewhere."** 一到两年内的结果，主要由大盘走势而非内在价值变化决定（Graham：短期是投票机，长期是称重机）。
- R28. **绝不用借来的钱买伯克希尔**：自 1965 年以来已三次从高点下跌约 **50%**；**"Someday, something close to this kind of drop will happen again, and no one knows when."**
- R29. 财务存续需要三条同时在位：**可靠的巨大盈利流、庞大的流动资产、无重大近期现金需求**。第三条最常被忽略。
- R30. 保守的原因不是悲观，而是时间不可知：**"it is entirely predictable that people will occasionally panic, but not at all predictable when this will happen. Though practically all days are relatively uneventful, tomorrow is always uncertain. (I felt no special apprehension on December 6, 1941 or September 10, 2001.)"**
- R31. 拒绝低概率的毁灭性风险：**"We will never play financial Russian roulette with the funds you've entrusted to us, even if the metaphorical gun has 100 chambers and only one bullet. In our view, it is madness to risk losing what you need in pursuing what you simply desire."**
- R32. 文化的自我复制是系统的一部分：**"Our system is also regenerative. To a large degree, both good and bad cultures self-select to perpetuate themselves."**

**芒格抽出的"Berkshire system"（15 条原文结构，见下节）**
- R33. 系统的价值不在单条而在"集合"：**"Both the elements of the Berkshire system and their collected size are quite unusual. No other large corporation I know of has half of such elements in place."**
- R34. 设计意图可以事后被诊断出来，但不必事前被预言：**"Buffett stumbled into some benefits through practice evolution. But, when he saw useful consequences, he strengthened their causes."**（这是全书级的机制描述：**看见好结果，就加固它的成因。**）
- R35. 良性循环的机制：**给予自主权 → 系统成功知名 → 吸引更好的子公司与 CEO → 更好的子公司需要总部更少关注** → "virtuous circle"。
- R36. 用任期长度来对齐后果：**"He wanted decisions that maximized long-term results, seeking these from decision makers who usually stayed long enough in place to bear the consequences of decisions."**（让决策者活到承受后果的那一天。）
- R37. 信任的前置条件：**"In choosing CEOs of subsidiaries, Berkshire would try to secure trustworthiness, skill, energy, and love for the business and circumstances the CEO was in."**（注意第四条：**热爱其所处的生意与处境**。）
- R38. 收益留存政策的一句话判据：**"Berkshire would not pay dividends so long as more than one dollar of market value for shareholders was being created by each dollar of retained earnings."**
- R39. 永远不要换 CEO 的赛道：**"Berkshire would almost never transfer a subsidiary's CEO to another unrelated subsidiary."** 并且**"Berkshire would never force the CEO of a subsidiary to retire on account of mere age."**
- R40. 永不卖掉子公司：**"As an important matter of preferred conduct, Berkshire would almost never sell a subsidiary."**
- R41. 现金收购优于发股：因为**"it was hard to get anything in exchange for Berkshire stock that was as valuable as what was given up."**
- R42. 交易保密：**"No one but the Chairman and one or two others at Berkshire would ever know about the offer if it did not lead to a transaction. And they would never tell outsiders about it."**
- R43. 对官僚主义的比喻级判断：**"the worst attributes of bureaucracy should much more often be treated like the cancers they so much resemble."** 并以**乔治·马歇尔（George Marshall）**为例：**"he helped win World War II by getting from Congress the right to ignore seniority in choosing generals."**

## 三、【悖论】PARADOX

- P1. **【常识认为】**八分之一美元（0.125）微不足道。**【实际】**1964 年这场八分之一点的抠门，让巴菲特拒绝出售、转而买入，最终让 Seabury Stanton 失业、让巴菲特把 BPL 超过 25% 的资本困在一门烂生意里二十年，并且——按巴菲特自己的估算——**把大约 $100 billion 从 BPL 合伙人手里转给了一群陌生人**。**【因为】**激起的那口气，买下的不是股票，是一整套后续命运。
- P2. **【常识认为】**赚钱靠聪明，聪明靠天生。**【实际】**"In 56 years, however, we've never had an argument."——最长寿的合伙人关系建立在**不争吵**而非不分歧上。**【因为】**芒格每次分歧都用一句话结束：**"Warren, think it over and you'll agree with me because you're smart and I'm right."** 把"攻击观点"完全置换成了"交付信任"。
- P3. **【常识认为】**买好公司要出好价钱，便宜买一般公司更划算。**【实际】**芒格给的蓝图恰好相反：**"Forget what you know about buying fair businesses at wonderful prices; instead, buy wonderful businesses at fair prices."**——巴菲特原以为自己在"买便宜的普通生意"，而这一改变才让伯克希尔既做大又不降低回报。**【因为】**雪茄烟蒂的便宜是**一次性的**、不可规模化的：**"Cigar-butt investing was scalable only to a point."** 且"**Selecting a marriage partner clearly requires more demanding criteria than does dating.**"
- P4. **【常识认为】**好生意值得出高价，See's 家族要 $30 million 就该给。**【实际】**巴菲特出 $25 million，因为"three times net tangible assets made me gulp"，差点毁掉这笔生意——而 See's 后来累计税前赚了 **$1.9 billion**，只需追加投资 **$40 million**。**【因为】**一家公司最值钱的资产可能**看不见**：**"the company had a huge asset that did not appear on its balance sheet: a broad and durable competitive advantage that gave it significant pricing power."**
- P5. **【常识认为】**伯克希尔的账面价值就是它的价值，因为过去一直如此。**【实际】**今天**"the gap between Berkshire's intrinsic value and its book value has materially widened."** 且保险、受监管业务两块的内在价值与账面价值的差距"far greater. It is there that the truly big winners reside."**【因为】**买入的整体企业**永不被向上重估**，无论价值涨了多少。
- P6. **【常识认为】**负债就是负债，$1 的浮存金和 $1 的欠款一样。**【实际】**"Owing $1 that in effect will never leave the premises – because new business is almost certain to deliver a substitute – is worlds different from owing $1 that will go out the door tomorrow and not be replaced. **The two types of liabilities are treated as equals, however, under GAAP.**"**【因为】**会计不区分"周转资金"与"即将到期的债务"。巴菲特称浮存金应被看作**revolving fund**（循环基金）而非负债，且它被全额从账面价值中扣除。
- P7. **【常识认为】**浮存金是免费的钱，越大越好。**【实际】**"float is valuable, its benefits can be drowned by poor underwriting results."——整个产险业长期承保亏损，**"This loss, in effect, is what the industry pays to hold its float."** 所以行业整体长期回报低于美国企业平均水准。**【因为】**所有保险公司都想要同一个好结果，这种竞争如此激烈，本身就摧毁了那个结果；**"at Berkshire it is a religion"**（在伯克希尔，那条消息是宗教）。
- P8. **【常识认为】**风险 = 波动率，低波动 = 安全。**【实际】**"Over the long term, however, currency-denominated instruments are riskier investments – far riskier investments – than widely-diversified stock portfolios."；**"Volatility is far from synonymous with risk."** 六年前听信"安全"建议去买国库券/大额存单的人，如今在**他们原以为供养退休的积蓄上赚着微薄的利息**（那时 S&P 500 低于 700，现在约 2,100）。**【因为】**商学院的教科书把波动当风险的代理变量——"makes for easy teaching, **it is dead wrong**"。且讽刺的是：**害怕无意义的价格波动，会让人做出真正危险的事。**
- P9. **【常识认为】**多元化集团（conglomerate）对投资者是坏东西。**【实际】**"Conglomerates, it should be acknowledged, have a terrible reputation with investors. And they richly deserve it."——但**伯克希尔恰恰是这种形式，而且它是"an ideal structure for maximizing long-term capital growth"**。**【因为】**资本主义的好处是资本会被高效配置，但现实中有巨大摩擦：**纺织业的资金撤出被管理层的虚荣与私利拖延了几十年**（1954 年的董事会纪要就是证据）；个人层面有税与摩擦成本；机构层面有一堆"昂贵的嘴"要喂（投行、会计师、咨询、律师、LBO 操作者）。集团结构能在**不缴税、几乎无摩擦**的前提下把巨额资金从机会有限的老业务挪到有新机会的领域。而且**"We have made plenty of mistakes, and we will make more."** 形式本身不是保障。
- P10. **【常识认为】**CEO 会为股东利益把资金从衰退行业撤出来。**【实际】**"A CEO with capital employed in a declining operation seldom elects to massively redeploy that capital into unrelated activities. A move of that kind would usually require that long-time associates be fired and mistakes be admitted."**【因为】**而且"it's unlikely that CEO would be the manager you would wish to handle the redeployment job even if he or she was inclined to undertake it."——**能做这个决定的人，恰恰不是该去执行它的人。**
- P11. **【常识认为】**并购创造价值，协同效应是真的。**【实际】**巴菲特做了 19 家公司的董事，**"I've never heard 'dis-synergies' mentioned, though I've witnessed plenty of these once deals have closed."**；投行在董事会里从不量化"你给出的股票内在价值 vs 你收到的企业内在价值"，只谈"惯例溢价"——**"an absolutely asinine way to evaluate the attractiveness of an acquisition"**。**【因为】**收费先于交易：**"fees too often lead to transactions rather than transactions leading to fees."** 同一批银行家，先劝买方付 20%–50% 溢价买"控制权价值"，几年后又板着脸劝你把同一块业务分拆出去"释放股东价值"——**"So much for that talented CEO we met earlier."**
- P12. **【常识认为】**发股票并购是"做大"的常规手段。**【实际】**以高价股票连续并购的商业模式**不创造财富，只重新分配财富**——**"just like chain-letter models – most assuredly redistribute wealth, but in no way create it."** "The ending is always the same: Money flows from the gullible to the fraudster. And with stocks, unlike chain letters, the sums hijacked can be staggering."**【因为】**1960 年代的"20 倍市盈率买 10 倍市盈率 + pooling 会计"是一个闭环骗局：每股收益自动上升，然后这个上升被当成管理天才的证据，用来维持高市盈率，好继续下一轮。而这些被买的低市盈率公司，**characteristically mediocre businesses with poor long-term prospects**——**越并购，底层的资产质量越垃圾。**
- P13. **【常识认为】**保险公司卖保单。**【实际】**"Simply put, insurance is the sale of promises."——**卖的是一句话，而兑现日可能在 50 年后。** 【因为】因此唯一关键的是"**both the ability and willingness of the insurer to pay, even if economic chaos prevails when payment time arrives**"。全世界只有 8 张单一保费超过 $10 亿的财险保单，**全部出自伯克希尔**——因为在那种尺度上，能接电话的只有一家。
- P14. **【常识认为】**低信用分、低收入的借款人风险高。**【实际】**"**Our blue-collar borrowers, in many cases, proved much better credit risks than their higher-income brethren.**" Clayton 在萧条的 2008-2009 年保持了良好的组合表现，**"a very high percentage of our borrowers kept their homes."**；而宾夕法尼亚大道上的高收入者在同一年大批弃房。**【因为】**仓位大小与还款的认真程度，不完全由收入或 FICO 决定。
- P15. **【常识认为】**2008 年危机中商业银行和保险公司都在收缩。**【实际】**"we continued during that period to finance our competitors' retail sales as well as our own."（Clayton 在别人断粮时给**竞争对手**的零售业务放贷。）**【因为】**危机中唯一还能放贷的人，就是提前攒了 $20 billion 现金且不欠短期债的人。
- P16. **【常识认为】**现金是拖累资产负债表的无生产力资产（拖累 ROE）。**【实际】**"Cash, though, is to a business as oxygen is to an individual: never thought about when it is present, the only thing in mind when it is absent."——2008 年 9 月，许多长期繁荣的公司**一夜之间开始怀疑自己的支票会不会被退回**；"Overnight, their financial oxygen disappeared."**【因为】**"Too often, CEOs of profitable companies feel they will always be able to refund maturing obligations, however large these are." 而上一个说这话的时代是 2008。
- P17. **【常识认为】**买伯克希尔这么安全的公司，什么价都不会亏。**【实际】**"a sound investment can morph into a rash speculation if it is bought at an elevated price. **Berkshire is not exempt from this truth.**" 若买价接近两倍账面价值，可能需要很多年才能赚钱。同时：**自 1965 年以来，股价曾三次从高点下跌约 50%。** 【因为】一家好公司与一笔好投资是两回事。
- P18. **【常识认为】**CEO 应当趁年轻赶快做出业绩，65 岁退休让位。**【实际】**"Berkshire will operate best if its CEOs average well over ten years at the helm. **It's hard to teach a new dog old tricks.**" 芒格对典型大公司的描述是：**"a long succession of CEOs who come in at about age 59, pause little thereafter for quiet thought, and are soon forced out by a fixed retirement age."** 【因为】长期在任者才**活得到品尝自己决策后果的那一天**。
- P19. **【常识认为】**投资能力会在年老时衰退（像运动员）。**【实际】**芒格：**"Buffett succeeded for the same reason Roger Federer became good at tennis."**——但**"Buffett much out-Woodened Wooden, because in his case the exercise of skill was concentrated in one person, not seven, and his skill improved and improved as he got older and older during 50 years, instead of deteriorating like the skill of a basketball player does."** 【因为】篮球教练 **John Wooden** 的赢球法是**把几乎全部上场时间交给最好的七个人**——对手永远面对你的最佳阵容，而因上场时间更多，最好的球员进步得比正常更快。巴菲特把这一机制压缩到了一个人身上，并让它运行了 50 年，因此复利不是作用于资本，而是作用于**技能**。
- P20. **【常识认为】**一个 34 岁的人不可能按自己的意愿塑造一家大公司。**【实际】**芒格：**"Buffett, even when only 34 years old, controlled about 45% of Berkshire's shares and was completely trusted by all the other big shareholders. He could install whatever system he wanted. And he did so, creating the Berkshire system."** 【因为】**系统的安装权来自信任，而信任来自持股与人格，两者在那一刻同时到位。**
- P21. **【常识认为】**保险公司长期只能产生平庸回报（"even when very well managed"）。**【实际】**芒格：**"Berkshire's better outcome was so astoundingly large that I believe that Buffett would now fail to recreate it if he returned to a small base while retaining his smarts and regaining his youth."** 【因为】前面几十年的超额收益来自"保险子公司的股票组合跑赢指数"，后来这个优势**因规模与税负而 fade to insignificance**；但**另一些更好的优势出现了**（Ajit Jain 从无到有造出巨额再保险、GEICO 市场份额翻两番、声誉带来的私人配售机会）。
- P22. **【常识认为】**股东要求分红是天然诉求。**【实际】**2013 年会上，一份讥讽式提案（"the owners unlike Warren are not multi billionaires"）被：**A 股 89:1 否决；B 股 660,759,855 否 对 13,927,026 是，约 47:1**——**"98% of the shares voting said, in effect, 'Don't send us a dividend but instead reinvest all of the earnings.'"** 【因为】一群自选的、与管理者同频的股东，会主动选择延迟满足。
- P23. **【常识认为】**错误应该被遗忘、成绩应该被宣传。**【实际】**巴菲特在五十周年长文里，用了最长、最详细的篇幅去写自己一生最贵的三个错误（NICO 买错主体、Waumbec、Dexter），并给出量化的伤害（**$100 billion**、**$5.7 billion**）。**【因为】**芒格把最大的错误界定在另一处：**"while mistakes of commission were common, almost all huge errors were in not making a purchase, including not purchasing Walmart stock when that was sure to work out enormously well... Berkshire's net worth would now be at least $50 billion higher if it had seized several opportunities it was not quite smart enough to recognize as virtually sure things."**——**最昂贵的错误是"没买"，而"没买"从不留下痕迹。**

## 六、附：芒格《副主席的思考——过去与未来》（The Berkshire System 全文骨架）

芒格开篇自陈五项目标：(1) 描述让一家注定失败的小宗商品纺织企业长成强大伯克希尔的**管理系统与政策**；(2) 说明系统从何而来；(3) 解释为何做得这么好；(4) 预测若巴菲特很快离去、异常好的结果是否会延续；(5) 思考过去 50 年是否对别处有用。

### "the Berkshire system" 原文十五条
1. 伯克希尔将是一个**分散型集团**，唯一回避的是无法做出有用预测的活动。
2. 顶层公司几乎全部通过**独立注册的子公司**做生意，子公司 CEO 以**极度自主**的方式运营。
3. 集团总部几乎什么都没有，只有一间**小办公室**，装着一位董事长、一位 CFO 和几位主要帮 CFO 做审计、内控等的助理。
4. 子公司中**必须始终包含财产意外险公司**，这群保险商将在适当时机产生**可靠的承保盈利**，并产生可供投资的**巨额 float**。
5. **不设全系统的人事系统、股票期权系统、其他激励系统、退休系统**等，因为各子公司有自己的系统，且往往互不相同。
6. 董事长只保留少数几项活动：
   (i) 管理几乎全部证券投资，通常放在财险子公司内；
   (ii) 挑选所有重要子公司的 CEO，定其薪酬，并从每人取得**一份私密的继任者推荐**，以备突发；
   (iii) 在子公司已增强其竞争优势之后，配置子公司不需要的大部分现金，**理想用途是收购新子公司**；
   (iv) 让自己**随时可被任何子公司 CEO 找到**，同时几乎不要求额外联系；
   (v) 写一封**长、有逻辑、有用的信**放进年报，写法要像自己是被动股东时希望读到的那样；并在年会上花数小时答疑；
   (vi) 努力成为某种**文化上的典范**，这种文化能长期对客户、股东及其他参与者有效——在他离开之前和之后都有效；
   (vii) 首要优先级是**为安静阅读和思考留出大量时间**，尤其是推进其坚定学习的那种阅读，无论他多老；
   (viii) 还要花大量时间**热情地欣赏别人正在成就的事情**。
7. 新子公司通常用**现金**买，不用新发股票。
8. **只要留存的一美元能创造超过一美元的市场价值，就不分红。**
9. 买新子公司时，为**董事长能相当好地理解**的好生意付**公道价格**；同时要有已在位的好 CEO，此人预期长期留任，且无需总部帮助即可管好。
10. 挑选子公司 CEO 时，争取**可信、技能、精力，以及对其生意与处境的热爱**。
11. 作为一项重要的偏好行为：**几乎从不卖掉子公司。**
12. **几乎从不把子公司 CEO 调到另一个不相关的子公司。**
13. **永不因年龄本身**强迫子公司 CEO 退休。
14. 保持**很少的未偿债务**，以维持 (i) 任何条件下的近乎完美信用，(ii) 在异常机会出现时现金与信贷的易得性。
15. 对可能出售大生意的卖家**永远用户友好**：报价会得到即时关注；若未成交易，除董事长和另外一两人外无人知晓，且他们绝不外传。

### 芒格对系统起源的判断
- **"Buffett, even when only 34 years old, controlled about 45% of Berkshire's shares and was completely trusted by all the other big shareholders. He could install whatever system he wanted. And he did so, creating the Berkshire system."**
- 几乎每条都因巴菲特相信"在他手下能最大化伯克希尔的成就"而选，**不是**为了给别的公司造一个"放之四海而皆准"的系统；子公司也**不被要求**采用这套系统，有些用别的系统照样繁荣。

### 芒格诊断出的五个设计意图
1. 持续最大化系统中**最重要的人**（从他自己开始）的**理性、技能与投入**。
2. 处处追求**双赢**——例如"**in gaining loyalty by giving it**"（通过给出忠诚来获得忠诚）。
3. 追求**长期结果最大化**，并且要求决策者**在位足够久、能承受决策后果**。
4. 把总部大官僚机构必然带来的坏影响**最小化**。
5. **像本·格雷厄姆教授那样，亲自为智慧的传播作出贡献。**

### 芒格对"为何做得这么好"的四因素
(1) 巴菲特的**建设性怪癖**；(2) 伯克希尔系统的**建设性怪癖**；(3) **好运**；(4) 某些股东与其他仰慕者（**包括一些媒体人士**）那种**怪异而强烈的、具传染性的虔诚**。
**"But the heavy freight was carried by the constructive peculiarities, the weird devotion, and their interactions."**

### 芒格对"巴菲特走后会怎样"的回答
**是。** 理由：子公司拥有大量**基于持久竞争优势的商业动能**；铁路与公用事业子公司提供了**在固定资产上投入巨额资金的可欲机会**；许多子公司在做明智的 bolt-on。
**"Provided that most of the Berkshire system remains in place, the combined momentum and opportunity now present is so great that Berkshire would almost surely remain a better-than-normal company for a very long time even if (1) Buffett left tomorrow, (2) his successors were persons of only moderate ability, and (3) Berkshire never again purchased a large business."**
但在这个假设下，继任者**不会是"只有中等能力"**：**Ajit Jain 和 Greg Abel** "would probably be under-described as 'world-class.' 'World-leading' would be the description I would choose. **In some important ways, each is a better business executive than Buffett.**" 且他相信这两人**无论如何都不会离开伯克希尔，也不会想对系统做大改动**。
他还认为**收购不会随巴菲特离去而终止**：**"With Berkshire now so large and the age of activism upon us, I think some desirable acquisition opportunities will come and that Berkshire's $60 billion in cash will constructively decrease."**

### 芒格对"别处可否复用"的回答
**可以。** 早期伯克希尔的任务是"把一小笔钱变成一家大而有用公司"，解法是**回避官僚主义、长期依赖一位深思熟虑的领导者**，让他不断提升并引入更多像他一样的人。
对照典型大公司：**"much bureaucracy at headquarters and a long succession of CEOs who come in at about age 59, pause little thereafter for quiet thought, and are soon forced out by a fixed retirement age."**
**"I believe that versions of the Berkshire system should be tried more often elsewhere and that the worst attributes of bureaucracy should much more often be treated like the cancers they so much resemble."**
例证：**乔治·马歇尔**通过从国会取得**无视资历挑选将军**的权力，帮助赢得了二战。
